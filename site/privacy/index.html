Privacy policy

How Stroke Lab handles your data

Effective 6 September 2026. Stroke Lab runs in your browser. We do not receive your strokes or reports.

What the lab processes

The lab processes pointer timestamps and coordinates while you draw. It uses coordinates to show raw and smoothed traces.

Timing totals stay in the current tab. Closing the tab discards the session.

What exports contain

JSON exports contain timing totals, findings, browser capabilities, and your notes. Coordinates are excluded by default.

Pressure and tilt are also excluded by default. Each option applies only to strokes made after you select it.

Your browser downloads the report directly. Review the file before sharing it.

Storage and network use

We use no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cloud stroke storage, or third-party scripts.

A service worker caches public site files for offline use. Remove that cache through your browser's site-data controls.

The sample demo uses memory only. It does not read or change a real test.

Your choices

Select “Clear test” to remove the current measurements. Delete an exported report with your device's file manager.

Contact

For a privacy request, open an issue in the Stroke Lab repository (external).

Do not include sensitive drawing content in a public issue.
