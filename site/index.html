How it works

Check a stroke in three steps

  1. 01 Load or drawUse the sample or make strokes with a pen, mouse, touch, or keyboard.
  2. 02 Compare delaysRead input, smoothing, render, and undo findings with measured reasons.
  3. 03 Export JSONDownload the timing report and attach it to a bug report.
Timestamp limits

Browsers can round timestamps. The report states its estimate. Each diagnosis is a lead, not proof of a driver fault.

Privacy and limits

What Stroke Lab does not do

  • It does not upload strokes, reports, or notes.
  • It does not inspect tablet drivers or replace a brush engine.
  • Exports omit coordinates, pressure, and tilt by default.
